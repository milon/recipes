Here is how to make a delicious flattened-rice dessert with just a few simple ingredients.

## Ingredients

- Flattened rice (chira) (150 g)
- Milk (1/2 liter)
- Sugar (2 tablespoons)
- Apple (1)
- Banana (1)
- Dates (4–5)
- Dried apricots (3–4)
- Raisins (for serving)
- Cashews (1 tablespoon)

![Flattened rice dessert](/assets/images/recipes/chirar-dessert.jpg)

## Method

First, soak the flattened rice in water for 30 minutes, then drain. In a pot, heat the milk with the sugar, then let it cool. Slice the banana and apple. Add the cold milk to the soaked flattened rice. Top with the sliced banana and apple. Garnish with sliced dates, raisins, chopped cashews, and dried apricots, then serve.
