No festival, including Eid, feels complete without semai. Here is a recipe for laccha semai.

## Ingredients

- Laccha semai (250 g)
- Milk (1 liter)
- Sugar (1 cup)
- Cardamom (2)
- Cinnamon (2 sticks)
- Bay leaf (1)
- Raisins (for serving)
- Cashews (for serving)

![Laccha semai](/assets/images/recipes/laccha-semai.jpg)

## Method

First, in the dish you will serve the semai in, lightly break apart the laccha semai from the packet and arrange it. In a pot, heat the milk with the sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, and bay leaf. When it comes to a boil, pour the milk over the laccha semai and sprinkle chopped raisins and cashews on top. The amount of milk may seem like a lot, but within a few minutes the semai will absorb it all. Serve once it has cooled slightly.
