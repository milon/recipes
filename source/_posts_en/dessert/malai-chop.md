Here is how to make malai chop very easily using milk powder.

## Ingredients

- Milk powder (1 cup)
- Egg (1)
- Baking powder (1/4 teaspoon)
- Sugar (2 cups)
- Cardamom (5–6)
- Ghee (1 tablespoon)
- Milk (1.5 liters)
- Saffron (1/4 teaspoon)
- Chopped almonds (for serving)

![Malai chop](/assets/images/recipes/malai-chop.jpg)

## Method

First, combine the milk powder and baking powder in a pot with the ghee and a well-beaten egg. Knead it well for 10–15 minutes. Make sure the dough is very smooth and lump-free. Shape the sweets into diamond (chamcham) forms. Keep in mind that these sweets will double in size after cooking, so shape them accordingly.

In a pot, heat 1.5 liters of milk with 2 cups of sugar, a little saffron, and a few cardamom pods. When the milk comes to a boil, add the sweets. Cook over high heat for 5 minutes. Then cook over medium heat for another 20 minutes. Once cooled, sprinkle chopped almonds and saffron on top and serve.
