Here is how to make semolina halwa, a classic breakfast item.

## Ingredients

- Semolina (1 cup)
- Sugar (1/2 cup)
- Chopped nuts (1 tablespoon)
- Milk (1/2 liter)
- Raisins (for serving)
- Cardamom (2)
- Cinnamon (2 sticks)
- Bay leaf (1)

![Semolina halwa](/assets/images/recipes/sujir-halua.jpg)

## Method

First, toast the semolina lightly in a pan over medium heat with the cardamom, cinnamon, and bay leaf. Transfer it to a separate pot.

In a pot, heat the milk with the sugar. When it comes to a boil, add the toasted semolina. Then add the raisins. When the milk has reduced, remove from the heat. Serve topped with chopped nuts.
