Nothing compares to lassi on a hot day or at iftar. Here is a very easy recipe.

## Ingredients

- Sweet yogurt (2 tablespoons)
- Cold water (as needed)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Crushed ice (for serving)

![Lassi](/assets/images/recipes/lacchi.jpg)

## Method

Blend the yogurt with cold water until smooth. Add sugar to taste if you like. Serve chilled over crushed ice.
