On a scorching day, a glass of lemon sherbet can be pure relief. Here is the recipe.

## Ingredients

- Paper lemons (2)
- Water (500 ml)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Crushed ice (for serving)

![Lemon sherbet](/assets/images/recipes/lebur-sorbot.jpg)

## Method

Squeeze the juice from 2 paper lemons into a bowl. Dissolve sugar to taste in 500 ml of water. Add the lemon juice and stir well. Serve ice cold over crushed ice.
