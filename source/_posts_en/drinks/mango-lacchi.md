Nothing compares to lassi on a hot day or at iftar—and mango lassi is even better. Here is a very easy recipe.

## Ingredients

- Sweet yogurt (2 tablespoons)
- Mango (100 g)
- Cold water (as needed)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Crushed ice (for serving)

![Mango](/assets/images/recipes/mango.jpg)

![Mango lassi](/assets/images/recipes/mango-lacchi.jpg)

## Method

Blend the yogurt, diced mango, and cold water until smooth. Add sugar to taste if you like. Serve chilled over crushed ice.
