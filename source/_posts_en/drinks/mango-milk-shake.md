Mango milkshake is one of my favorite drinks. Here is the recipe.

## Ingredients

- Milk (3/4 glass or 200 ml)
- Mango (1/2 cup)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Ice (for serving)

![Mango](/assets/images/recipes/mango.jpg)

![Mango milkshake](/assets/images/recipes/mango-milk-shake.jpg)

## Method

Heat the milk, then chill it completely. Add the milk to a blender along with the mango pieces. Blend well with sugar to taste. You can skip the sugar if you prefer.

Serve over crushed ice.
