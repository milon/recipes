Rooh Afza is a very refreshing drink. Here is how to make sherbet with it.

## Ingredients

- Rooh Afza (2 tablespoons)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Lemon juice (1 teaspoon)
- Crushed ice (for serving)

![Rooh Afza sherbet](/assets/images/recipes/ruh-afzar-sorbot.jpg)

## Method

In a bowl, combine 500 ml of water with 2 tablespoons of Rooh Afza, 1 teaspoon of lemon juice, and sugar to taste. Stir well. Serve ice cold over crushed ice.
