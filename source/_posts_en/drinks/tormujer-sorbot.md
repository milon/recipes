Watermelon sherbet is wonderfully refreshing in the heat. Here is a very easy way to make it.

## Ingredients

- Watermelon (150 g)
- Water (100 ml)
- Sugar (to taste)
- Crushed ice (for serving)

![Watermelon sherbet](/assets/images/recipes/tormujer-sorbot.jpg)

## Method

Blend diced watermelon with cold water until smooth. Add sugar to taste if you like. Serve chilled over crushed ice.
