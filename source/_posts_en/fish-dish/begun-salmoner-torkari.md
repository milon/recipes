Here is a recipe for salmon curry with potato and eggplant.

## Ingredients

- Eggplant (2)
- Potato (2)
- Salmon (2 fillets, 250 g)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)

![Salmon curry with eggplant](/assets/images/recipes/begun-salmoner-torkari.jpg)

## Method

First, cut and wash the salmon, rub lightly with turmeric, and shallow-fry over low heat. Set aside.

Cube the eggplant and potato and wash them. Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. Then add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt. Add the potato and eggplant and cook covered. The moisture released from the eggplant should be enough to soften both vegetables; add a little water only if needed.

Add the fried salmon and green chilies and cook 2–3 minutes more. Serve.
