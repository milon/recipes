Here is a quick look at how to make chapila fish bhuna.

## Ingredients

- Chapila fish (500 g)
- Chopped onion (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (2 teaspoons)
- Chili powder (2 teaspoons)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (5–6)
- Salt (to taste)

![Chapila fish bhuna](/assets/images/recipes/chapila-mach-vuna.jpg)

## Method

First, scale the chapila fish, rub lightly with salt and turmeric, and shallow-fry.

Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. When the onion softens, add all the spices except the green chilies and fry well, adding a little water if needed. Add the fried chapila fish and cook for 5–7 minutes. Just before serving, add green chilies sliced lengthwise.
