Here is a recipe for delicious prawn dopeyaja.

## Ingredients

- Prawns (500 g)
- Chopped onion (2 cups)
- Turmeric powder (2 teaspoons)
- Chili powder (2 teaspoons)
- Coriander powder (1 teaspoon)
- Cumin powder (1 teaspoon)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (5–6)
- Salt (to taste)

![Prawn dopeyaja](/assets/images/recipes/chingrir-dopeyaja.jpg)

## Method

First, clean and wash the prawns well, rub lightly with turmeric, shallow-fry, and set aside.

Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. When the onion softens, add all the spices except the green chilies and fry well, adding a little water if needed. Add the fried prawns and cook for 5–7 minutes. Just before serving, add green chilies sliced lengthwise.
