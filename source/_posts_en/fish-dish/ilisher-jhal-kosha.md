Hilsa is a favorite for all of us. Here is a distinctive recipe made with hilsa fish.

## Ingredients

- Hilsa fish (1 medium)
- Chopped onion (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (2 teaspoons)
- Chili powder (2 teaspoons)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (5–6)
- Kaffir lime leaves (3–4)
- Salt (to taste)

![Hilsa jhal kosha](/assets/images/recipes/ilisher-jhal-kosha.jpg)

## Method

First, cut and wash the hilsa, rub lightly with salt and turmeric, fry, and set aside.

Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. When the onion softens, add all the spices except the kaffir lime leaves and green chilies, and fry, adding a little water if needed so nothing burns. When the spices are ready, add the hilsa and whole green chilies. Cook for 5–7 minutes. One or two minutes before taking the pot off the heat, add the kaffir lime leaves. Serve hot.
