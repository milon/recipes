In Germany and across Europe, small round red radishes are a very common vegetable. Here is a prawn curry made with them.

## Ingredients

- Red radish (500 g)
- Tomato (4)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Prawns (20–25)
- Salt (to taste)

![Radish and prawn curry](/assets/images/recipes/mula-chingrir-torkari.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the radish and slice it thinly. Wash the tomatoes, cut them into small pieces, and set aside. Dust the prawns with a pinch of turmeric and shallow-fry them lightly in oil. Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. Then add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt. Add the radish and tomato, pour in enough water, and cover. The radish will soften quickly. Add the fried prawns and green chilies, stir for 2–3 minutes, and serve.
