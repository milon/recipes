Malabar spinach with prawns is one of my favorite curries. Here is the recipe.

## Ingredients

- Malabar spinach (500 g)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Prawns (20–25)
- Salt (to taste)

![Malabar spinach and prawns](/assets/images/recipes/pui-chingri.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the malabar spinach well and cut it as needed. You can halve any large leaves down the middle. Dust the prawns with a pinch of turmeric and shallow-fry them lightly in oil. Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. Then add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt. Add the malabar spinach with a little water and cover. The greens will wilt and shrink quickly. Add the fried prawns and green chilies, stir for 2–3 minutes, and serve.
