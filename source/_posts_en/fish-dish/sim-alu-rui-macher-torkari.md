Rui fish curry with winter vegetables makes a wonderful dish. Here is an easy recipe to learn.

## Ingredients

- Rui fish (6 pieces)
- Tomato (3)
- Hyacinth beans (10–15)
- Potato (2)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Salt (to taste)
- Cilantro (for serving)

![Rui fish curry with hyacinth beans and potato](/assets/images/recipes/sim-alu-rui-macher-torkari.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the rui fish pieces, rub lightly with turmeric, shallow-fry in oil, and set aside.

Heat oil in a pot and add chopped onion. When the onion softens, add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt. Add medium-sized cubed tomato, peeled hyacinth beans cut in half, and long potato slices. Stir lightly and add 1 cup water. The potato and beans will soften quickly and the tomato will break down into a coating gravy. Add the fried fish and green chilies and cook 2–3 minutes more. Finish with cilantro and serve.
