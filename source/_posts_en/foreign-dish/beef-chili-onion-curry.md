Let's look at how to make the delicious beef chili onion curry found at Chinese restaurants.

## Ingredients

- Beef (250 grams)
- Carrot (1)
- Onions (4 large)
- Green chilies (5–6)
- Corn flour (1 tablespoon)
- Curry powder (1 teaspoon)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (2 tablespoons)
- Tomato sauce (2 tablespoons)
- Soy sauce (2 teaspoons)

![Beef chili onion curry](/assets/images/recipes/beef-chili-onion-curry.jpg)

## Method

Cut the beef into thin slices. Heat oil in a pot and add turmeric powder, chili powder, curry powder, and salt. Add the beef and a little water if needed. Cook until the beef is tender.

When the beef is done, add thinly sliced carrot and green chilies. Peel 4 large onions and cut each into 4 wedges. Add them to the pot and cook for a few minutes. Then add tomato sauce, soy sauce, salt to taste, and 1 tablespoon of corn flour dissolved in a little water. Stir and cook until the gravy reduces and becomes thick. Serve hot.
