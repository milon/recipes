I got this recipe from my Croatian colleague Jan. It is very popular across the Balkans in Europe. Let's look at the recipe—it is essentially a skinless sausage, also known as ćevapi.

## Ingredients

- Ground beef (500 grams)
- Chopped onion (1)
- Chopped garlic (1 teaspoon)
- Black pepper powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Baking soda (1/2 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)

![Ćevapčići](/assets/images/recipes/cevapcici.jpg)

## Method

Mix all ingredients except the oil thoroughly in a bowl. Take portions of the mixture and shape them into long sausage-like kebabs.

Heat oil in a pan and fry the kebabs over medium heat. You can also grill them if you prefer. Serve hot.
