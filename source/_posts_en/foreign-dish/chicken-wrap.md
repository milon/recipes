Let's learn how to make a delicious chicken wrap snack.

## Ingredients

- Boneless chicken (200 grams)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Black pepper powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Ginger-garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Tomato sauce (1 teaspoon)
- Soy sauce (1 teaspoon)
- All-purpose flour (1 cup)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for deep-frying)

![Chicken wrap](/assets/images/recipes/chicken-wrap.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the chicken and cut it into small pieces. Marinate with chili powder, salt, black pepper powder, ginger-garlic paste, tomato sauce, and soy sauce for 30 minutes.

Meanwhile, make a dough with flour, salt, and enough water. Roll it out and cut into long strips about 1.5 inches wide. Wrap each strip around a piece of chicken and secure with a toothpick. Deep-fry in hot oil until the chicken is fully cooked through. Serve hot.
