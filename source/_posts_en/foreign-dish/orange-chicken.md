Orange chicken is one of the most popular dishes at the American restaurant chain Panda Express. Here is how to make it at home.

## Ingredients

- Chicken breasts (2)
- Orange juice (1 cup)
- Soy sauce (1 tablespoon)
- Sugar (1/2 cup)
- All-purpose flour (1/2 cup)
- Cornstarch (1 tablespoon)
- Egg (1)
- Salt (to taste)
- MSG (to taste)
- Finely chopped garlic (1 teaspoon)
- Finely chopped ginger (1 teaspoon)
- Sesame seeds (1 teaspoon)
- Chili flakes (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for deep-frying)

![Orange chicken](/assets/images/recipes/orange-chicken.jpg)

## Method

Wash the chicken and cut it into small cubes. Add salt, a little soy sauce, half the garlic, and the egg, then marinate for 30 minutes. Coat the chicken with the flour and 2 teaspoons of the cornstarch. Deep-fry until golden.

For the sauce, heat a little oil in a wok or frying pan. Add the chili flakes, ginger, and remaining garlic. Fry for about a minute, then add the orange juice, remaining soy sauce, sugar, and MSG. Mix the remaining 1 teaspoon of cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of water and add it to thicken the sauce. Once thickened, add the fried chicken and toss until evenly coated. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, stir, and serve.
