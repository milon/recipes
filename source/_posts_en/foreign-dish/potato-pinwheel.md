Let's look at how to make delicious potato pinwheels.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes (3)
- All-purpose flour (1 cup)
- Egg whites (2)
- Semolina (1 tablespoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Black pepper powder (1 teaspoon)
- Panch phoron powder (1 teaspoon)
- Roasted cumin powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (1 tablespoon, plus more for deep-frying)


![Potato pinwheel before frying](/assets/images/recipes/potato-pinwheel.jpg)

![Potato pinwheel](/assets/images/recipes/potato-pinwheel-fried.jpg)

## Method

In a bowl, mix flour, semolina, and salt to taste. Add 1 tablespoon of oil and enough water to make a dough similar to roti dough. Rub with a little oil, cover, and rest for 1 hour.

Boil the potatoes, peel them, and mash. Mix in panch phoron powder, cumin powder, black pepper powder, chili powder, turmeric powder, and ginger paste until well combined.

Divide the dough into four portions and roll each out like a paratha. Spread the potato mixture evenly on top. Roll up tightly from one edge into a long log, then slice into pinwheels.

Dip the pinwheels in egg white and deep-fry until golden brown. Serve hot.
