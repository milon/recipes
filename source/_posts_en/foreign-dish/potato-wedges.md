Let's look at how to make delicious potato wedges.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes (4)
- All-purpose flour (1/2 cup)
- Rice flour (1/4 cup)
- Corn flour (1/4 cup)
- Salt (to taste)
- Dried chilies (2)
- Chopped cilantro (1 tablespoon)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for deep-frying)

![Potato wedges](/assets/images/recipes/potato-wedges.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the potatoes well and cut them into large wedges with the skin on. Make sure each wedge has three corners. Bring a large pot of water to a boil with 1 tablespoon of salt. Add the potatoes and cook until about 70–80% done. Drain and let cool.

In a bowl, combine rice flour, corn flour, and all-purpose flour with salt to taste and mix well. Add dried chili flakes, turmeric powder, chili powder, garlic powder, and chopped cilantro and mix again. Add water to make a batter—it should be fairly thin. When you dip a potato wedge, it should get only a light coating.

Dip the par-cooked wedges in the batter and deep-fry until golden brown. Serve hot.
