Here is how to make the kind of garlic sprout chicken you would find in a Chinese restaurant.

## Ingredients

- Chicken breast (2)
- Garlic sprouts (400 g)
- Chopped garlic (1 tablespoon)
- Soy sauce (1 tablespoon)
- MSG (1 teaspoon)
- Chili flakes (1 teaspoon)
- Corn flour (1 teaspoon)
- Sesame seeds (1 pinch)
- Salt (to taste)

![Garlic sprout chicken](/assets/images/recipes/garlic-sprout-chicken.jpg)

## Method

Cut the chicken into small pieces and marinate with chili flakes, salt, and corn flour for 30 minutes. Cut the garlic sprouts into 2-inch pieces and blanch them in hot water for 1–2 minutes.

Heat a little oil in a large wok and fry the chicken. When it starts to color, add chopped garlic and stir briefly. Add the garlic sprouts, soy sauce, and MSG. Stir-fry everything on high heat for 3–4 minutes. Sprinkle sesame seeds over the top just before serving.
