Here is how to make delicious kathi kabab.

## Ingredients

- Beef mince (1 kg)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Cumin powder (1 teaspoon)
- Coriander powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Garam masala powder (2 teaspoons)
- Salt (to taste)
- Green chilies (4–5)
- Chopped onion (1/2 cup)
- Chopped cilantro (1 teaspoon)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)

![Kathi kabab](/assets/images/recipes/kathi-kabab.jpg)

## Method

Mix the beef mince with all the spices until very well combined. Press the mince around skewers into long kabab shapes. Deep-fry the skewered kababs in hot oil and serve immediately.
