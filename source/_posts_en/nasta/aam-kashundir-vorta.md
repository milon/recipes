Here is a delicious recipe for green mango mash with kasundi.

## Ingredients

- Green mangoes (3 / 250 g)
- Green chilies (3–4)
- Salt (to taste)
- Kasundi (2 tablespoons)
- Sugar (1 tablespoon)

![Green mango and kasundi mash](/assets/images/recipes/aam-kashundir-vorta.jpg)

## Method

Peel the green mangoes, wash them well, and cut them into thin slices. Mash them together with chopped green chilies, salt, sugar, and kasundi. Serve immediately.
