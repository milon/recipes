Iftar never feels complete without potato chop. Here is an easy recipe for making it at home.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes (2 large)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Finely chopped onion (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (2)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Potato chop — before frying](/assets/images/recipes/alur-chop-kacha.jpg)

![Potato chop — after frying](/assets/images/recipes/alur-chop-vaja.jpg)

## Method

First, boil the potatoes. Mash them with salt, green chilies, and chopped onion. Shape the mash into small chops like in the photos.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Deep-fry the chops in medium-hot oil after dipping them in the batter. Serve hot.
