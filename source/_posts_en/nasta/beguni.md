Beguni is an essential part of iftar. Here is an easy recipe for making it at home.

## Ingredients

- Long eggplant (1)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Finely chopped onion (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (2)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Beguni](/assets/images/recipes/beguni.jpg)

## Method

Wash the eggplant and slice it thinly lengthwise.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon salt, chopped onion, and chopped green chilies to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Deep-fry the eggplant slices in medium-hot oil after dipping them in the batter. Serve hot.
