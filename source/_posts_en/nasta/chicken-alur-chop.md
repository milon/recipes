Here is a recipe for potato chop filled with spiced chicken mince.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes (2 large)
- Chicken mince (1 cup)
- Finely chopped onion (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (2)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Red chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chopped cilantro (1 teaspoon)
- Ginger-garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garam masala (1 teaspoon)
- Breadcrumbs (1 cup)
- Egg (1)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Chicken potato chop](/assets/images/recipes/chicken-alur-chop.jpg)

## Method

Mix the chicken mince with turmeric powder, red chili powder, chopped cilantro, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, and salt. Fry the mince in a pan with a little oil until cooked through.

Boil the potatoes and mash them with salt, chopped green chilies, the fried chicken mince, and chopped onion. Shape the mash into small chops like in the photo. Beat an egg in a bowl. Dip each chop in the egg, roll in breadcrumbs, and deep-fry over medium heat until golden brown. Serve hot.
