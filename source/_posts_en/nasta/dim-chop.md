Here is a recipe for classic Bengali egg chop.

## Ingredients

- Eggs (4)
- Potatoes (2–3 large)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Finely chopped onion (1 teaspoon)
- Green chilies (2)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Egg chop](/assets/images/recipes/dim-chop.jpg)

## Method

Boil the potatoes and mash them with salt, green chilies, and chopped onion. Hard-boil the eggs and peel them. Wrap each egg in a thin layer of potato mash, making sure no egg shows through.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Deep-fry the chops in medium-hot oil after dipping them in the batter. Serve hot.
