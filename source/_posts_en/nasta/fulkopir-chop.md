Here is an easy recipe for delicious cauliflower chop.

## Ingredients

- Cauliflower (1 head)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Red chili powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Cauliflower chop](/assets/images/recipes/fulkopir-chop.jpg)

## Method

Cut the cauliflower into large florets and wash them. Boil them in salted water until about 80% tender, then drain and set aside.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder, and 1 teaspoon salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Dip the cauliflower florets in the batter and deep-fry over medium heat until golden brown. Serve hot.
