Here is a simple recipe for a delicious grilled cheese sandwich.

## Ingredients

- Bread
- Cheese
- Butter
- Black pepper powder

![Grilled cheese sandwich](/assets/images/recipes/grilled-cheese-sandwich.jpg)

## Method

Butter one side of a slice of white sandwich bread. Place it butter-side down in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Add a slice of cheese. Processed cheese is traditional for grilled cheese — I used American processed cheddar. Sprinkle a little freshly ground black pepper on top. Cover with another slice of bread and butter the top slice as well. Cook until both sides are golden brown. Serve hot.
