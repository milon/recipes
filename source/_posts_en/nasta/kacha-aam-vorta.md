Here is a mouthwatering recipe for green mango mash.

## Ingredients

- Green mangoes (3 / 250 g)
- Green chilies (3–4)
- Salt (to taste)
- Sugar (1 tablespoon)

![Green mango mash](/assets/images/recipes/kacha-aam-vorta.jpg)

## Method

Wash the green mangoes, peel them, and finely chop or grate them on the coarse side of a box grater. Mash them together with chopped green chilies, salt, and sugar. Serve immediately.
