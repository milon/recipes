Here is a recipe for a distinctive green chili chop.

## Ingredients

- Green chilies (10–12)
- Lemon juice (1/2 cup)
- Soy sauce (1/2 cup)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Green chili chop](/assets/images/recipes/kacha-moricher-chop-2.jpg)

![Green chili chop](/assets/images/recipes/kacha-moricher-chop-1.jpg)

## Method

Wash the green chilies and pierce them several times with a toothpick. Be careful not to split them open — keeping them whole gives a better-looking chop. Soak them in a mixture of soy sauce and lemon juice for 1 hour.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Deep-fry the soaked chilies in medium-hot oil after dipping them in the batter. Serve hot.
