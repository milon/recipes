Here is a recipe for delicious fritters made with kachki fish.

## Ingredients

- Kachki fish (250 g)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Finely chopped onion (2 cups)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Red chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Cumin powder (1 teaspoon)
- Lemon juice (1 tablespoon)
- Egg (1)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Cilantro (to taste)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for frying)

![Kachki fish fritters](/assets/images/recipes/kachki-macher-bora.jpg)

## Method

Wash the kachki fish thoroughly and drain well. Small fish often carry a lot of grit, so rinse them several times. Mix all the remaining ingredients together in a bowl.

Heat oil in a pan. When the oil is hot, drop spoonfuls of the mixture into the oil and fry until golden brown. Serve hot.
