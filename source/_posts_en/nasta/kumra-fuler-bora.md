Here is a recipe for delicious pumpkin blossom fritters.

## Ingredients

- Pumpkin blossoms
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Red chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Black pepper powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Pumpkin blossom fritters — before frying](/assets/images/recipes/kumra-fuler-bora-before-frying.jpg)

![Pumpkin blossom fritters — after frying](/assets/images/recipes/kumra-fuler-bora.jpg)

## Method

Remove the pistils and stems from the pumpkin blossoms, then wash and drain them well. For the batter, add turmeric powder, red chili powder, black pepper powder, and salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a batter slightly thinner than pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Dip the blossoms in the batter and deep-fry over medium heat until golden. Serve hot.
