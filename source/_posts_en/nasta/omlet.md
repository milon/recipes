An omelet is a breakfast staple. Here is an easy recipe for a delicious one.

## Ingredients

- Eggs (2)
- Finely chopped onion (1 tablespoon)
- Chopped scallions (1 teaspoon)
- Chopped red bell pepper (1 teaspoon)
- Chopped green bell pepper (1 teaspoon)
- Chopped yellow bell pepper (1 teaspoon)
- Chopped green chilies (2)
- Salt (to taste)
- Butter (50 g)

![Egg omelet — while cooking](/assets/images/recipes/omlet-1.jpg)

![Egg omelet](/assets/images/recipes/omlet-2.jpg)

## Method

Combine all the chopped vegetables in a bowl with salt to taste and mash them lightly with your hands. Crack in 2 eggs and mix well.

Melt butter in a pan. Pour in the egg mixture and cook over medium heat. When one side sets, flip and cook the other side. Serve hot.
