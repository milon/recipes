Here is an easy recipe for crispy fried papad.

## Ingredients

- All-purpose flour (1 cup)
- Water (2 cups)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for frying)

![Fried papad](/assets/images/recipes/papor-vaja.jpg)

## Method

In a bowl, mix the flour with salt and water until smooth. Use twice as much water as flour.

Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Place a stand inside the pot. Lightly oil a small plate, add a little of the flour mixture, and set the plate on the stand. Cover the pot. After a few minutes the mixture on the plate will set. Transfer it to a large tray lightly rubbed with oil. Repeat until all the mixture is used to make thin papad sheets. Dry them thoroughly — you can also dry them in the sun.

Finally, deep-fry each papad. They cook very quickly, usually in 15–20 seconds. Serve hot.
