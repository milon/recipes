Here is an easy recipe for tomato chop.

## Ingredients

- Tomatoes (2)
- Gram flour (besan) (1 cup)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Oil (for frying)
- Salt (to taste)

![Tomato chop](/assets/images/recipes/tomator-chop.jpg)

## Method

Wash the tomatoes and slice them into thin rounds.

For the batter, add 1 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon salt to the gram flour. Add enough water to make a mixture the consistency of pancake batter. Make sure there are no lumps. Deep-fry the tomato slices in medium-hot oil after dipping them in the batter. Serve hot.
