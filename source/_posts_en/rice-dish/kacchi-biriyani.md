Here is an easy and delicious kacchi biryani recipe.

## Ingredients

- Mutton (1 kg)
- Potatoes (4–5)
- Polao rice (4 cups)
- Oil (1 cup)
- Green chilies (6–7)
- Sour yogurt (250 g)
- Salt (to taste)
- Onion (1 cup)
- Cardamom (4)
- Cinnamon (4 pieces)
- Cloves (4)
- Bay leaves (2)
- Ginger paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garlic paste (1 teaspoon)
- Garam masala (2 teaspoons)
- Turmeric powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Coriander powder (1 teaspoon)
- Cumin powder (1 teaspoon)
- Nutmeg (1)
- Mace (2–3 pieces)
- Ghee (2 tablespoons)
- Saffron (1 teaspoon)
- Kewra water (1 teaspoon)
- Milk (1/2 cup)
- Flour (for sealing the pot)

![Kacchi biryani](/assets/images/recipes/kacchi-biriyani.jpg)

## Method

Wash the mutton, cut it into small pieces, and marinate with a little oil, 1 cup sour yogurt, turmeric powder, chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, nutmeg-mace paste, ginger-garlic paste, and salt for 4–5 hours. Overnight marination for 8+ hours works best.

Fry the chopped onion until golden brown and set the beresta aside.

Peel and wash the potatoes, rub lightly with salt and turmeric, and deep-fry until golden. Set aside.

Boil enough water in a pot and parboil the polao rice until about 80% cooked. Drain and set aside.

In the pot you will cook the biryani in, layer the marinated mutton at the bottom. Top with the parboiled rice and the fried potatoes. Add fried onion, a few spoonfuls of ghee, kewra water, and saffron soaked in 1/2 cup milk for 30 minutes. Seal the pot well with a flour dough. Place it on the stove over full heat for 10 minutes. Then, without breaking the seal, bake in the oven at 180°C (350°F) for 1.5 hours.

Remove the biryani from the oven, break the seal, mix the rice with the mutton, and serve hot.
