Most of us eat salad from time to time. Here is an easy recipe for making one at home.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber (1)
- Tomato (2)
- Carrot (1)
- Iceberg lettuce (1 cup)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Salt (to taste)

![Salad](/assets/images/recipes/salad.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the tomatoes and cucumber and slice them thinly. Peel the carrot and cut it into long strips. Wash the iceberg lettuce and cut it into small pieces. Finely chop the onion and green chilies.

In a bowl, add the chopped onion and green chilies with a little salt and rub them together with your fingers. Add the remaining ingredients, toss well, and serve.
