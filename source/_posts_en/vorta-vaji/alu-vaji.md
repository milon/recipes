Most of us already know how to make potato stir-fry. I'll add a small twist to bring out an exceptional flavor. Let's get started.

## Ingredients

- Potato (500 grams)
- Tomato (2)
- Onion (1)
- Cilantro (for serving)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Potato stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/alu-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, peel and dice the potatoes, then rinse well. Dice 2 tomatoes into small pieces and set them aside with the potatoes. The tomato adds umami and gives the stir-fry a beautiful color.

Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion turns lightly golden, add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt, and stir once. Add the tomatoes and potatoes and keep stirring. Add a little water if needed and cover. The potatoes will soften and the tomatoes will break down within a few minutes. Split the green chilies and add them. Stir a couple of times and sprinkle cilantro just before serving.

This potato stir-fry is wonderful with hot paratha.
