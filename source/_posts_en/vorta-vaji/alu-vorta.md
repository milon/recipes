Here is a simple, everyday potato mash recipe.

## Ingredients

- Potato (500 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Cilantro (for serving)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Potato mash](/assets/images/recipes/alu-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash and boil the potatoes until tender. Peel and mash them well. Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion turns lightly golden, add the mashed potato, green chilies, cilantro, and salt to taste. Stir-fry until done, then take off the heat and serve.
