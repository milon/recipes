Here is how to make fried eggplant rounds easily.

## Ingredients

- Eggplant (1)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for frying)

![Eggplant fry](/assets/images/recipes/begun-vaja.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the eggplant and cut it into thick rounds. Coat lightly with turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt, then deep-fry in hot oil until done. Serve hot.
