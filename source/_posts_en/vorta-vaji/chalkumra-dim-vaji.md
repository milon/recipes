Here is how to make an easy ash gourd and egg stir-fry.

## Ingredients

- Ash gourd (1)
- Eggs (2)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Ash gourd and egg stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/chalkumra-dim-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the ash gourd, peel it, and dice it. You can also grate it if you prefer. Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. Add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt, stir well, then add the ash gourd. Keep stirring, then cover after a few minutes.

You do not need to add extra water — the ash gourd will release plenty of moisture and cook in its own juices. Wait until the liquid dries up. When it is nearly dry, add a few green chilies split down the middle. Break in 2 eggs and mix well. Stir for a few minutes until the eggs are fully cooked, then take off the heat.
