Living abroad, you always crave dried fish mash. Here is how to make chepa dried fish mash easily.

## Ingredients

- Chepa dried fish (3–4)
- Onion (3–4)
- Dried chilies (7–8)
- Salt (to taste)

![Chepa dried fish mash ingredients](/assets/images/recipes/chepa-sutki-vorta-2.jpg)

![Chepa dried fish mash](/assets/images/recipes/chepa-sutki-vorta-1.jpg)

## Method

First, rinse the dried fish well, rub with salt, and cook in a pan with a very small amount of water until the liquid dries up — by then the fish should be tender. Take it off the heat and mash with dried chilies in a mortar and pestle. Peel 3–4 onions and mash them thoroughly. Mix the mashed onion with the fish mash and serve.
