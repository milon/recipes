Bitter melon is a very healthy food. Here is a different take — crispy bitter melon fry.

## Ingredients

- Bitter melon (250 grams)
- Rice flour (1 tablespoon)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for frying)

![Crispy bitter melon fry](/assets/images/recipes/crispy-korolla-vaja.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the bitter melon and cut it into moderately thick rounds. Mix in turmeric powder, chili powder, rice flour, and salt until evenly coated. Deep-fry in hot oil until crisp.

Serve hot with rice.
