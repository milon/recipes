Here is a healthy red amaranth stir-fry recipe.

## Ingredients

- Red amaranth (500 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Garlic (3–4 cloves)
- Dried chilies (3–4)
- Salt (to taste)

![Red amaranth stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/data-saak-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the red amaranth well and chop it finely. Boil until tender and drain thoroughly.

Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion, minced garlic, and dried chilies broken into pieces. When the onion softens, add the greens and salt to taste. Stir-fry well for 1–2 minutes, then take off the heat and serve.
