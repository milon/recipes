Here is how to make okra stir-fry very easily.

## Ingredients

- Okra (250 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Okra stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/dheros-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the okra and cut it into small pieces. Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion softens slightly, add salt to taste. Add the cut okra and keep stirring continuously. Okra cooks quickly and does not take long. Do not cover the pan — that will ruin the color of the okra. Add green chilies split down the middle 1–2 minutes before taking off the heat.
