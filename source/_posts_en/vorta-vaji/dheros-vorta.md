Here is how to make okra mash very easily.

## Ingredients

- Okra (250 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Dried chilies (4–5)
- Salt (to taste)
- Mustard oil (1 teaspoon)

![Okra mash](/assets/images/recipes/dheros-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the okra and cut it into medium-sized pieces. Boil the okra until tender and drain well. Mash it thoroughly and set aside in a bowl.

Heat oil in a pan and fry the dried chilies until crisp. Mash together diced onion, mustard oil, fried dried chilies, and salt. Add the prepared okra and mix until well combined.
