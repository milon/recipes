Here is how to make egg mash easily.

## Ingredients

- Eggs (3)
- Onion (1)
- Dried chilies (2–3)
- Salt (to taste)
- Mustard oil (1 teaspoon)

![Egg mash](/assets/images/recipes/dim-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, boil the eggs, peel them, and grate them. If you do not have a grater, you can chop or mash them by hand. In a bowl, mash together diced onion, fried dried chilies, salt, and mustard oil. Add the eggs and mix well. Serve hot.
