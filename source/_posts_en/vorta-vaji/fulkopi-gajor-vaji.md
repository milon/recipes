Here is how to make a tasty mixed vegetable stir-fry with cauliflower and carrot.

## Ingredients

- Cauliflower (1 head)
- Carrot (3)
- Potato (1)
- Green beans (1/2 cup)
- Tomato (2)
- Onion (1 large)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (2 tablespoons)

![Cauliflower and carrot stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/fulkopi-gajor-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash all the vegetables except the tomatoes and cut them into small pieces. Wash the tomatoes, dice them into small cubes, and set aside in a separate bowl.

Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion turns lightly golden, add turmeric and chili powder and stir. Add all the vegetables, stir well, pour in about 1 cup of water, and cover. The vegetables will cook through and the liquid will evaporate within a few minutes. Season with salt to taste. Add the diced tomatoes and a few green chilies split down the middle, and cook for another 3–4 minutes. Remove from the heat and serve hot.
