Here is how to make a tasty spiny gourd fry easily.

## Ingredients

- Spiny gourd (2–3)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (for frying)

![Spiny gourd fry](/assets/images/recipes/kakrol-vaja.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the spiny gourd, peel it, and cut into long strips. Coat lightly with turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt, then deep-fry in hot oil until done. Serve hot.
