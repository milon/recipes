Here is how to make a delicious jackfruit seed mash.

## Ingredients

- Jackfruit seeds (250 grams)
- Dried chilies (4–5)
- Dried shrimp (100 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Garlic (5–6 cloves)
- Mustard oil (to taste)
- Salt (to taste)
- Cilantro (to taste)

![Jackfruit seed mash](/assets/images/recipes/kathaler-bichi-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, shell the dried jackfruit seeds and roast them well in a griddle or skillet. Roast over low heat for a longer time so the centers are fully cooked.

Heat mustard oil in a pan and fry the dried shrimp, diced onion, garlic, dried chilies, and salt to taste until fragrant. Add the jackfruit seeds midway through frying.

Transfer the fried mixture and fresh cilantro to a blender or food processor and blend until smooth. Your delicious jackfruit seed mash is ready. Serve hot with rice.
