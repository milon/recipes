Here is how to make a simple white bitter melon stir-fry.

## Ingredients

- Bitter melon (250 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)
- Green chilies (2–3)

![Bitter melon stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/korolla-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the bitter melon and cut it into thin rounds. Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion softens slightly, add the bitter melon. Season with salt to taste. When the stir-fry is nearly done, add 2–3 green chilies split down the middle. Serve hot.
