Here is how to make a tasty bottle gourd stir-fry with eggs.

## Ingredients

- Bottle gourd (1 medium)
- Eggs (2)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Scallion (1)

![Bottle gourd stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/lau-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, peel, wash, and dice the bottle gourd into small cubes. Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion softens slightly, add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt. Add the bottle gourd pieces and stir-fry well. The gourd will release water, and it will cook in its own juices. Once the liquid dries up, break in 2 eggs and mix thoroughly. Just before taking off the heat, add chopped scallion. Serve hot.
