Here is how to make a tasty butternut squash mash.

## Ingredients

- Butternut squash (250 grams)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1)
- Salt (to taste)
- Cilantro (to taste)
- Mustard oil (to taste)

![Butternut squash mash](/assets/images/recipes/misti-kumrar-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the butternut squash, cut it into small pieces, and boil until tender. Take off the heat and mash well.

In a bowl, mash together diced onion, diced green chilies, and chopped cilantro with salt and mustard oil. Add the mashed squash and mix until well combined.
