Here is a quick look at a traditional vegetarian taro greens recipe.

## Ingredients

- Taro greens with stems (500 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Garlic (15–20 cloves)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Dried chilies (2–3)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Vegetarian taro greens](/assets/images/recipes/niramish-kochu-shak.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the taro greens with stems and cut them into small pieces. Put 1/4 cup of water in a pot along with garlic cloves, green chilies, taro greens, and salt to taste, and steam over the heat. The taro will soften within a few minutes and reduce considerably in volume. Mash the taro well with a spatula. Once the water dries up, transfer to a bowl and set aside.

Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion and dried chilies. When the onion turns golden, add turmeric and chili powder and sauté briefly. Add the cooked taro and stir-fry over medium heat for a long time until almost no moisture remains. When the taro greens are fully stir-fried, take off the heat and serve hot with rice.
