Here is a wonderfully tasty malabar spinach stir-fry.

## Ingredients

- Malabar spinach (500 grams)
- Dried chilies (3–4)
- Onion (1)
- Garlic (2–3 cloves)
- Salt (to taste)

![Malabar spinach stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/paat-saak-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the malabar spinach. Put it in a large pot over the heat — within a short while it will wilt down considerably and cook through. Set the cooked spinach aside.

Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion, minced garlic, salt, and dried chilies. When the onion is nearly fried, add the spinach. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes, then take off the heat.
