Here is an easy and tasty hyacinth bean and potato stir-fry recipe.

## Ingredients

- Potato (250 grams)
- Hyacinth beans (250 grams)
- Onion (1)
- Cilantro (for serving)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Turmeric powder (1 teaspoon)
- Chili powder (1 teaspoon)
- Salt (to taste)
- Oil (1 tablespoon)

![Hyacinth bean and potato stir-fry](/assets/images/recipes/sim-alu-vaji.jpg)

## Method

First, peel and dice the potatoes. Dice the hyacinth beans as well. Mix the potato and bean pieces together, rinse well, and drain.

Heat oil in a pan and add diced onion. When the onion turns lightly golden, add turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt, and stir once. Add the potatoes and beans and keep stirring. Add a little water if needed and cover. The beans and potatoes will soften within a few minutes. Split the green chilies and add them. Stir a couple of times and sprinkle cilantro just before serving.

This hyacinth bean and potato stir-fry is wonderful with hot roti or steaming rice.
