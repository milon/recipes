Here is how to make a tasty hyacinth bean and potato mash.

## Ingredients

- Hyacinth beans (250 grams)
- Potato (2 large)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1 large)
- Cilantro (2 tablespoons)
- Mustard oil (1 tablespoon)
- Salt (to taste)

![Hyacinth bean and potato mash](/assets/images/recipes/sim-alur-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the hyacinth beans and boil until tender. Mash them in a mortar and pestle. I do not have a mortar, so I blended them in a blender. Add onion, green chilies, and salt while blending.

Boil the potatoes separately, peel, and mash well. Transfer the bean mash from the blender, add a little mustard oil and chopped cilantro, mix in the mashed potato, and serve.
