Here is how to make a tasty hyacinth bean mash.

## Ingredients

- Hyacinth beans (250 grams)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1 large)
- Cilantro (2 tablespoons)
- Mustard oil (1 tablespoon)
- Salt (to taste)

![Hyacinth bean mash](/assets/images/recipes/sim-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the hyacinth beans and boil until tender. Mash them in a mortar and pestle. I do not have a mortar, so I blended them in a blender. Add onion, green chilies, and salt while blending.

Transfer from the blender, mix in a little mustard oil and chopped cilantro, and serve.
