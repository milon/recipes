Tomato is one of my favorite vegetables. Here is how to make tomato mash.

## Ingredients

- Tomato (4–5)
- Green chilies (2–3)
- Onion (1 large)
- Cilantro (2 tablespoons)
- Mustard oil (1 tablespoon)
- Salt (to taste)

![Tomato mash](/assets/images/recipes/tomato-vorta.jpg)

## Method

First, wash the tomatoes, dice them, and put them in a pan over the heat. Cook until fully softened — within a few minutes all the tomatoes will break down. Mash any large pieces with a spatula. When the tomato juices have reduced, transfer to a bowl.

In the bowl, add diced onion, chopped cilantro, diced green chilies, mustard oil, and salt, and mash together well. Combine thoroughly with the cooked tomatoes and serve.
